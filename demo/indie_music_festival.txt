Monsoon Indie Nights 2027 is a two-day independent music festival in Mumbai on 10 and 11 July 2027,
hosted by Monsoon Collective, a community of independent musicians. We expect 5,000 people, mostly college students and young professionals
aged 18 to 30. Sponsorship tiers: Presenting Partner at ₹25 lakh, Associate Partner at ₹8 lakh.
No tobacco or betting brands.
