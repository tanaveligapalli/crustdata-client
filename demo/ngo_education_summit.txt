Shiksha Forward Summit 2027 is a one-day education conference in Mumbai on 18 April 2027, hosted by
Sahayog Foundation, an NGO that runs after-school programmes. We expect 600 attendees: teachers,
school leaders and CSR heads. Sponsorship tiers: Lead Partner at ₹10 lakh, Supporting Partner at
₹4 lakh. No alcohol, tobacco or betting brands.
